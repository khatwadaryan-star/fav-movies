# Decisions:
One of the most important decisions I made during this project was choosing a movie recommendation website as my topic. Watching movies is one of my favorite pastimes, so it was easier to stay motivated and think of ideas for content with this topic in mind. I decided to focus on five of my favorite films: The Dark Knight, Interstellar, Inception, Oppenheimer, and Memento. Another important decision was expanding the project from a simple three-page website featuring a Home, Movies, and About page into a larger multi-page site with dedicated pages for each movie. This allowed me to provide more information about each film and create a more engaging experience for visitors.
# What worked:
The aspect of the project that worked best was the overall organization of the website. The navigation links via the navigation bar made it easy to move between the three main pages, and the separate movie pages contained a consistent and clear structure for presenting information about each film. I am also satisfied with the visual improvements that were made between Project 01 and Project 02, as the center-aligning of the text on each page and the transition from a monochrome background to a vibrant red background helped make the site more interesting and visually appealing.
# What I would do differently:
If I were starting over, I would spend more time planning the site's structure before writing code. Early on in the project, I focused mainly on getting the pages working rather than thinking about how the entire site would grow from there. As I added more features, I had to consistently revisit and reorganize parts of the HTML and CSS. A more detailed wireframe and content plan would have saved time spent in the later parts of the project.
# What I learned:
This project taught me that building a website involves much more than just simply displaying content. Seemingly small but important details such as spacing, alignment, navigation, and responsiveness have a major impact on the overall user experience. I also learned how important it is to test pages on different screen sizes due to how a design that looks good on one device may appear completely different on another. I also improved my skills with tools like HTML, CSS, GitHub, and GitHub Pages, as I grew more comfortable creating multiple pages, linking them together, styling content consistently, and committing changes to a live site. Through the revision process, I learned how to better interpret feedback from my teacher and peers in order to make meaningful improvements to the project instead of only fixing minor issues.
